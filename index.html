FAQ

Questions

Something else? Ask FocuzAI or email hello@focuznow.com.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi, Arc and other Chromium browsers. The install button picks the right store for the browser you're using.

Is the free plan actually free?

Yes. Blocking, focus sessions, planning, habits and Focus Forest are free with no time limit. Pro is optional.

What's Nuclear Lockdown?

You pick a duration and your blocklist is locked until it ends. There's no pause button and no way to switch it off early, which is the point.

Can I block Shorts but still use YouTube?

Yes. Platform blockers hide specific parts like Shorts, Reels or the home feed while search, subscriptions and DMs keep working.

Where is my data stored?

In your browser's local storage. If you create an account for sync or Pro, see the privacy policy for exactly what's stored.

Can I cancel Pro anytime?

Yes. Cancel from billing and you keep Pro until the end of the period, then drop back to Free.

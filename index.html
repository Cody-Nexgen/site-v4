FAQ

Questions, answered.

Something else? hello@focuznow.com or ask FocuzAI.

Which browsers does FocuzNow support?

FocuzNow is a browser extension for Chromium-based browsers — Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, and Vivaldi. The install button above picks the right store for your browser automatically.

Is it really free?

Yes. The Free plan includes the dashboard, focus sessions, essential site blocking, calendar, and lists — no credit card required. Pro adds advanced schedules, Nuclear Lockdown, full statistics, FocuzAI, progression, and social features for $8/month.

What is Nuclear Lockdown?

A deliberate, irreversible block. Choose a duration and every site on your blocked list stays blocked until the timer ends — there's no override button, by design.

Where is my data stored?

FocuzNow is local-first: focus activity, preferences, plans, and blocking configuration are designed to stay in your browser workspace. Features that need an account, like friends and focus rooms, only process what they need to work. See the privacy policy.

Can I block just part of a site?

Yes. Route and platform blockers remove things like YouTube Shorts or Instagram Reels while keeping the rest of the site usable — great when you need YouTube for a tutorial but not the feed.

How do I cancel Pro?

Anytime, from the billing page in your account. You keep Pro until the end of the period, then drop back to Free — your data stays put.
